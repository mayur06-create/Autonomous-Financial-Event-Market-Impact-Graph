RBI Raises Repo Rate by 25 bps to 6.75%; Maintains Withdrawal of Accommodation
Economic Times, February 8, 2023

MUMBAI — The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) raised the
benchmark repo rate by 25 basis points to 6.75% on Wednesday, in a 4-2 majority
decision, signalling continued resolve to bring CPI inflation sustainably to the
4% target.

Governor Shaktikanta Das said the MPC would maintain its stance of "withdrawal of
accommodation" as long as necessary. The decision was broadly in line with market
expectations, though a minority of analysts had forecast a pause.

Market reaction: Nifty Bank index fell 1.2% on the day as higher rates compress
net interest margins in the short term. The 10-year G-sec yield rose 7 basis points
to 7.42%. The Indian rupee strengthened marginally by 0.3% on improved real rate
differential expectations.

Sector implications:
- Banking & NBFCs: Transmission lag of 2–3 quarters means NIM pressure is temporary;
  asset quality remains the key variable.
- Real estate: Higher home loan rates (EMIs rise ~Rs 750/month per Rs 50 lakh loan)
  dampen affordability; residential demand may moderate in the H1 2023.
- Auto (two-wheelers): Retail credit constitutes ~70% of two-wheeler sales; rate
  sensitivity is high for entry-level segment.
- Infrastructure & Capital goods: Discount rate rise lowers NPV of long-duration
  projects.

The next MPC meeting is scheduled for April 2023. The market is pricing in a pause
at that meeting, but elevated global commodity prices remain a risk.
