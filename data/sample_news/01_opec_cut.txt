OPEC+ Announces Surprise 1 Million Barrel/Day Production Cut
Reuters, April 3, 2023

RIYADH — OPEC+ shocked global energy markets on Sunday by announcing a voluntary
production cut of approximately 1.16 million barrels per day (bpd) starting May 2023,
on top of the 2 million bpd reduction agreed in October 2022. Saudi Arabia alone will
cut 500,000 bpd. Russia extended its 500,000 bpd reduction through year-end.

Brent crude futures jumped more than 6% at the Asian open, trading above $86 a barrel.
Analysts at Goldman Sachs revised their year-end Brent forecast to $95/bbl.

Impact on India: India imports roughly 85% of its crude requirements. Every $10/bbl
rise in crude prices widens the current account deficit by approximately $15 billion
annually, according to RBI estimates. Oil marketing companies (BPCL, HPCL, IOC) may
face margin pressure if retail fuel prices are not raised; however, the government has
historically absorbed shocks close to elections to control inflation.

INR is expected to face depreciation pressure as the import bill rises. Aviation fuel
(ATF) constitutes 30–35% of airline operating costs, putting IndiGo, Air India, and
SpiceJet under pressure. Logistics companies such as Blue Dart and GATI will also see
cost headwinds.

The RBI, already monitoring sticky core inflation, may delay any rate cuts in the
near term. Bond yields are expected to tick up on rising inflation expectations.
